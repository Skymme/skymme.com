# Surface brief: skymme.com home (index.html)

Scope: the whole single page. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience: data science hiring managers screening a first-CDI Data Scientist candidate (secondary: HR). Job: judge credibility in seconds, then find depth. Action: download the CV or e-mail. Proof: real projects, six internships, INSA Data & AI specialisation, TOEIC 940. Constraints: static HTML readable without JS, light and dark themes, French.

Memorable moment: a floating "model run" card in the hero (a live forecast chart with its metrics) standing in for the reference's music player.

Pinned by the user: the style of https://uupm.cc/demo/music-streaming (Melodify demo), with an emerald + violet palette. A first "topographic survey" direction (seed e290e3f0) was built and rejected by the user; do not return to contour-map aesthetics.
